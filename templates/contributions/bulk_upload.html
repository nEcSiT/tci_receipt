Excel Upload Guidelines

  • Member Identifier: Enter Member Number (MEM-00000001) or phone number (e.g. 0241234567).
  • Category: Tithe, Offering, Welfare, Thanksgiving, Higher Life Partners, or Other.
  • Other Description: When category is "Other", plain-text purpose is required.
  • Welfare Coverage: Specify Year (e.g. 2026) and Months (e.g. "Whole Year" or "Jan, Feb, Mar"). Welfare has no fixed dues.
  • Receipts: Each row produces its own individual official receipt and personalized SMS upon confirmation.
