Linking Governance (PRD §5.6)

  • Member linking transfers all historical contributions to the target member profile.
  • Approval is strictly reserved for the System Administrator.
  • Financial audit trail, receipt numbers, and contribution records are preserved immutably.
  • Phone conflicts are flagged explicitly to prevent accidental identity collision.
  • Original temporary contributor records remain stored for provenance.
